Cycling 4 Climate is a Dutch not-for-profit-but-for-impact foundation, founded in 2019. The Glorious Glacier Ride carries riders 756 km and over 16,000 metres of climbing from Innsbruck to Chamonix, passing several retreating glaciers along the way, to raise awareness for climate change and fund local nature restoration and carbon-sinking projects.
